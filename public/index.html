      How to use Advisor Practice

      1. Choose a scenario, difficulty and time limit.
      2. Read the briefing and fictional training guidance.
      3. Select Start conversation and respond as the advisor.
      4. For voice practice, select Enable spoken replies, then Talk. Allow microphone access, speak, check the text and select Send reply. Typing always works. Use Mute customer to turn sound off.
      5. Select End & assess, or let the timer end the conversation.
      6. Review the scores, evidence and suggested improvements with a trainer.
      7. Download the report before the 24-hour retention period ends.

      Free AI has a shared daily allowance. If it runs out, the transcript remains available to download. Keep the Cloudflare account on the Free plan to avoid paid usage.
